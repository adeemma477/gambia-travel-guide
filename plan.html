Plan your trip

Five days on the Smiling Coast

A relaxed first-visit itinerary that mixes history, nature, local life and beach time, all within easy reach of the coastal resorts.

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Banjul city day

    Climb Arch 22, visit the National Museum and wander through Albert Market.

  2. Day 2

    Bakau and the beach

    Morning at Kachikally Crocodile Pool, then relax on the beach at Cape Point.

  3. Day 3

    Forest and wildlife

    Go birdwatching at Abuko Nature Reserve, then take a canoe ride through the mangroves at Makasutu.

  4. Day 4

    Roots river trip

    Take a boat up the River Gambia to Kunta Kinteh Island and the villages of Albreda and Juffureh.

  5. Day 5

    Fishing villages

    See the boats come in at Tanji, then finish the trip with a quiet afternoon at Sanyang beach.

When to go

The two main seasons in The Gambia
Season Months What to expect
Dry season November – May Sunny, little rain, the busiest time for visitors and good for birdwatching.
Rainy season June – October Hot and humid with heavy showers. The countryside is very green and there are fewer tourists.
